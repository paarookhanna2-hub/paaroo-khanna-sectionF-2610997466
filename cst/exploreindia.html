Famous Places in India

Taj Mahal – Agra

The Taj Mahal, built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal, is one of the Seven Wonders of the World. Its white marble beauty attracts millions of visitors every year.

Red Fort – Delhi

The Red Fort is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a symbol of India’s rich history. It was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan and is famous for its massive red sandstone walls.

Vrindavan – Prem Mandir

Vrindavan is a holy city associated with Lord Krishna. Prem Mandir is a stunning temple known for its intricate carvings and spiritual atmosphere.

Golden Temple – Amritsar

The Golden Temple, also known as Harmandir Sahib, is the holiest shrine of Sikhism. Its golden dome and serene lake make it one of the most beautiful places in India.

Hanuman Mandir – Delhi

The Hanuman Mandir is one of the oldest temples in Delhi, dedicated to Lord Hanuman. It is a popular pilgrimage site and is known for its spiritual energy.

Kedarnath Temple – Uttarakhand

Kedarnath Temple, located in the Himalayas, is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Surrounded by snow-capped peaks, it is a sacred destination for devotees and trekkers alike.
